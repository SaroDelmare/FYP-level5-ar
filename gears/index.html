    IT’S A REAL WALL

    Everything here is a physical part on the wall. Nothing needs power and no piece comes off.

    Push inEach gear slides on a fixed shaft. Push its knob and the gear lines up with its neighbours, so it turns with them.
    Pull outPull the knob and the gear slides forward, out of line. It spins on its own and passes nothing on.
    Turn the handleA ratchet lets it turn clockwise only, so you can’t cheat a challenge by cranking backwards.
    Watch the outputsThe two output gears have a blue stripe, so you can see which way they spin.
